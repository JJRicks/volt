# Roadmap

What's still planned for Volt. It's a plan, not a promise: the order can change and items can be dropped. Suggest something or offer help in [Issues](https://github.com/JJRicks/volt/issues), where new plans are discussed. What has shipped is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Languages

Volt ships in English and ten beta languages: Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), Dutch, Polish, Swedish and Turkish. Still to come:

- **The last text still written in the code** moves into the translation catalog. Until then, a few messages stay in English in every language.
- **More Latin-script languages:** Indonesian, then Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Czech, Slovak, Romanian, Hungarian and Croatian as reviewers come forward.
- **Other scripts, left to right,** only if they fit without disrupting the app: Russian, Ukrainian and Greek first, then Chinese (Simplified), Hindi and Bengali. These would use the phone's own fonts.
- **Right to left: on hold.** Arabic and Urdu need every screen mirrored, so they wait for now.

Native speakers are welcome to spot-check a beta language, especially its safety text: open an issue.

## Features

- **Reorder More's menu,** as the dashboard and the Scooter tab already can be.

## Investigating

- **Cruise control changes that come back "Not confirmed"** on some scooters. Volt should say more than that, but only once something the scooter reports shows the actual cause.

## Not planned

- Going beyond the speed limits that the scooter's firmware and the official NAVEE app allow. Volt never changes firmware.
