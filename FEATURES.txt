  of 25, 32, 40 and 50 km/h; limits for other UT5 models are not offered.
- Charge ceiling offers 80%, 85%, 90%, 95% and 100%. Stored schedule settings
  were verified; actual charging start/stop behavior needs a charging cycle.
- Low Battery Mode limits maximum speed below 20% charge. It is not an Eco mode
  that reduces power throughout an entire ride.
- Lock duration offers 6, 12 or 24 hours of locked standby. The OEM says the
  scooter can remain locked after powering off; this is not a guaranteed timed
  unlock. Keep your configured handlebar PIN available.
- Walk Assist follows walking speed when pushed in Walk mode. Power Assist uses
  the Boost button and full throttle uphill in Sport mode. Hill Descent Control
  limits speed while descending without braking. These are OEM descriptions;
  propulsion and descent behavior still need a rider check.

  model's normal and Boost colors are Light Blue and Red. Readback was verified;
  visual behavior has not been independently checked during this test run.
- Tail light replaces the incorrect Headlight label: opcode 0x54 maps to OEM TailLight

  for the headlight and tail light. Readback confirms configuration, not visible

  choosing a supported firmware estimator while fresh status confirms the
  scooter is stationary.
  Firmware without Eco support uses D-mode for Eco/Walk. Automatic attempts
  remain bounded across reconnects. This changes the scooter's prediction, not
  battery capacity or measured odometer; active Boost detection is unavailable.

STATS AND BATTERY DIAGNOSTICS

An earlier complete parked capture populated 83 of the 93 listed fields,
including response checks. A reconnect can initially supply fewer readings.
The app distinguishes missing and stale data instead of inventing zeroes.

Readings and Charts share search and collapsed categories. The numerical chart
catalog includes retained readings and current numerical values for the selected
scooter. A master picker offers 2 min through All, with all eight choices visible
together, including on narrow screens. Visible graphs update live and expand for
inspection. Stars add readings/charts to the Overview list. Its Reorder icon
opens a grab-handle sheet: dropping saves the order, cancelling restores it,
and accessible Move up/Move down actions remain available. The
Readings count remains; its redundant favourites filter is removed. History
resolution follows retention, older summaries are identified and gaps preserved.

Reported data includes:

- Charge, pack voltage/current, signed terminal power, temperature, reported
  health, charge counter, rated capacity and lifetime energy/capacity counters.
- Battery manufacturing date, deep-discharge events and temperature-related
  lifetime counters where the firmware supplies them.
- Speed, estimated remaining range, current trip, average/maximum speed,
  odometer and stored trips when driving/history reports arrive.
- Dashboard/controller/battery firmware, scooter identity, warning/status
  fields, raw non-secret diagnostic payloads and each reading's age.

Reported health and health-adjusted capacity are not independent battery tests.
Lifetime Wh/Ah counters are not current-ride consumption. Power is calculated
from paired voltage/current readings. Ride energy needs fresh samples and a
confirmed current direction before it can be called net battery consumption.

No decoded per-cell voltages, cell balance, motor/controller temperature,
torque, direct per-trip Wh or exact remaining pack Wh were found. Unknown fields
retain honest raw labels; firmware faults are not silently cleared.

Order: most likely to work first. Volt counts, from the official app's model
tables, how many related models (the same OEM model-ID family) and how many
other listed models offer each control; ties follow a fixed owner priority
(kick-start, cruise, energy recovery, traction, acceleration, turn sound, tail
light, automatic lights, units, then the rest). The family count is usually
zero, because the official app hides controls by family.

Help verify, a second group, lists the guided-test controls (dashboard units,
tail light, turn signal sound, automatic lights, range estimate) where the
official app offers them for the model and Volt has not verified them there
(OEM-mapped, not Volt-verified; an unidentified model: units only). Their
Scooter rows are unchanged; a try uses the same flow and conditions.

A try needs a fresh, owner-authenticated status with the field present and a
known value, the scooter stopped with a recent motion report, unlocked, no
scooter warning, no pending change, compatibility check, other try or ride or
charge recording. It runs like the former guided tests: a fresh baseline that
must match what the sheet showed, a journal entry on the phone before the
write, one write, and a readback that only replies to reads sent after the
acknowledgement can give. Then Volt asks "Did you see it change?" (Yes, No,
Skip). Results: "Worked here" (the new value read back), "No change seen"
(acknowledged, and a later read still showed the old value), "Refused by
scooter" (an error code), "Not sent" (refused by Volt before sending) or
"Other value read". Accepted but unconfirmed, or dropped after sending, is
never no change: the try stays unfinished for review (Restore, Keep or
Dismiss), and nothing is ever written again by itself after a disconnect or
restart. The value tried stays; Set back writes the earlier value in one tap,
journalled the same way, also after a restart. Controls the scooter does not
report are listed as not reported and cannot be tried.

Try records stay on the phone (at most 20 tries and tests, never in backups;
no serial, address, identity, PIN, names or times of day) until a report the
owner emails, shares or saves (once the file is written) includes them, or
until Clear try results; a later Set back or review waits for the next report. The
compatibility report (volt-compat-report-v1) lists them in an optional
"experimental" array (control, group, variant, layer, value before and tried,
result, step outcomes with times relative to the try, the owner's answer,
restoration, kept) and in summary lines.

APP TOOLS AND REMAINING VALIDATION

The app includes local profiles, appearance/text/unit choices, fold-aware
layouts, charts, ride recording, notes, comparison and exports. More uses saved
App, Scooters, Security, Lab, Data and About sections, showing one at a time without
rebuilding their controls. Submenus reset after one minute away from More or the
app, while active use and quick returns preserve the section. First-time owner setup stays visible when needed.

Keep idle connection in background defaults off. When off with no recording,
hiding the app or turning the screen off starts a 10-second grace period. If
still hidden without a recording, the deadline closes the idle session, cancels
reconnects and removes its notification. Failed connections and retries cannot
extend it.
Returning to the app cancels the deadline. Changing the option while already
hidden or stopping a hidden recording creates no new grace period. Active
ride/charge capture retains foreground-service protection and an immediate
ongoing Stop notification with a renewed bounded CPU wake lock. Charge capture
retries temporary disconnects for up to five minutes with marked gaps and
same-scooter verification; rides preserve Bluetooth gaps and can reconnect. A ride
ends automatically 10 s after Bluetooth is lost following fresh stationary
reports (preference, default on); moving or uncertain losses keep recording. Android may
allow dismissing ongoing notifications while unlocked. Force stop and device
battery restrictions remain limits. No lock state changes automatically.

Earlier emulator/render checks, parked recordings and Samsung startup
confirmation apply to the versions identified in VERIFICATION.txt. They do not
establish full physical validation of the current dashboard, phone sensors,
lighting output, folding transitions or ridden assistance behavior.

Ordinary diagnostics exclude owner identity and PIN digits. A private owner

RANGE LEARNED FROM YOUR RIDES

Scooter > Range estimate offers Scooter's estimate (default, following drive
mode) or Volt's estimate (learned), saved separately for each scooter. Volt's
estimate uses only that profile's completed ride recordings on this phone;
no other rider's range baseline ships with Volt.
With recorded power it forecasts battery energy left at the displayed level
(energy per gauge point learned in 10-point bands; 10% less than the least seen
below the lowest band ridden) divided by energy per km; the picker adds a likely
range from past 6 km stretches. Without power readings it divides total
accepted odometer distance by total battery-percentage points used.

Needs at least three usable rides, 20 battery points and 3 km within 90 days.
Every committed sample is checked in a streaming pass for movement, fresh
battery/distance endpoints, coverage, gaps, clock changes, charging and gauge
rebounds. With insufficient history, Volt's option is greyed but tappable for
a brief more-rides explanation. The first usable selection shows a short intro.
The picker distinguishes loading from an unreadable-history failure with retry;
it does not show a detailed evidence block. An unavailable learned estimate or
missing battery level leaves the scooter's estimate; a stale battery level
produces a stale estimate.

Applies to every current-range dashboard widget, including generic readings and
gauges, and the Live Stats overview range headline. Explicit Scooter range
readings/favourites, recorded charts and raw exports keep the reported source;
charts never mix a learned live value into previously recorded scooter values.
Choosing Volt's estimate changes local display only; choosing Scooter's estimate
enables guarded drive-mode following.
This is a preliminary estimate for similar riding, with no guaranteed distance
to empty or capacity/health claim. Hills, speed, payload, weather, rounding and
unobserved battery levels limit accuracy. See docs/validation/learned-range.md.

USABLE RIDE ENERGY (EXPERIMENTAL; FORMERLY CAPACITY EXPERIMENT)

Analyzes explicit rides for the selected scooter using:
  100 x summed recorded Wh / summed battery-percentage points used.
Confirmed-direction rides use net energy drawn minus returned. Unconfirmed
rides form a separate absolute-throughput proxy that also counts returned
energy and can overstate depletion. A percentage of advertised Wh is shown
when a reference is known; neither result is a battery-health measurement.

Rides need at least one minute, 30 seconds of measured movement, a five-point
battery drop, 95% electrical coverage, and aligned battery/power end readings.
Recording gaps/recovery, detected clock or current-direction changes, missing
endpoints and visible gauge rebounds over one point exclude a ride. Each
separate evidence group needs at least 60 accepted percentage points across six
rides before a result appears. With five or more rides, a ride more than 25%
from the median energy per point (plus one point of rounding) is set aside as a
gauge jump. Results show a 90% range from the ride-to-ride spread, the charge
per gauge (Ah), and a charging check from completed charge recordings. Sampled trends may miss brief rebounds. Active rides replace
their previous contribution and remain provisional.

Updates about every ten seconds while viewed. Uses a 90-day window by default;
7, 30, 90, 180, 365 and 730 days are selectable. Older recordings fall out of
the estimate without deletion. Widget options accept a custom advertised Wh;
zero uses a known model's default. UT5 MAX uses NAVEE's 716.04 Wh reference
(46.8 V x 15.3 Ah). Other models need a custom reference for a percentage.

This SOC-based energy equivalent depends on cold, load, voltage range and gauge
rounding/calibration. Available pack temperatures and edge-of-charge warnings
provide context without a temperature correction. Use ordinary riding without
charger input. The result cannot establish actual cell capacity or prove an
undersized pack. It starts no ride, Bluetooth connection, GPS or phone sensors.
README.md links official NAVEE specifications and TI's gauging reference.


CHARGING POWER, RECORDING AND ESTIMATES

Fresh charging status shows separate Charging power and Time to full tiles;
ordinary battery details return when charging is no longer freshly reported.
Power is battery-side absolute V x I from paired readings, not outlet power or
charger efficiency. Missing/stale/unpaired inputs leave readings unavailable.

Recognized UT5 MAX scooters get an experimental stock-charger reference without
recordings or Developer options. It combines rated-Ah/live-current bulk timing
with an observed finishing trajectory, including the rapid 91–95% gauge rise
and extended 99% taper. The finishing time is included before that range begins.
Only compatible live power enables the reference; this does not identify a
charger. Unknown models and incompatible readings keep the current-rate fallback.
Brief boundary fluctuations have bounded tolerance to avoid abrupt ETA switching;
stale readings and sustained or larger power mismatches still discard the reference.
Usable ride energy never changes charging estimates.

Scooter → Battery → Charger for estimates offers Stock / High-speed without
Developer options, including while disconnected. This per-scooter manual choice
changes estimates only: High-speed opts out of the bundled stock reference.
It neither sets charger output nor starts recording, and cannot change during
an active capture. The recording screen uses the same saved selection.

Explicit Charge recording lives in Developer options, with a manual Stock or
High-speed charger selection. There is no automatic charger detection. Bounded
local samples are saved every ten seconds. Temporary disconnects allow up to
five minutes of recovery with the same journal and marked gaps. Completion,
Stop, explicit Disconnect, recovery expiry or an ending/error condition closes it. Simulation cannot create training evidence.

Qualified recordings learn curves for each scooter/charger label. One continuous
recording reaching displayed 100% can provide a provisional finishing estimate
within its fully observed range. Other learned ranges need two usable recordings.
Personal estimates require compatible live power at the current battery level;
missing or incompatible evidence keeps the applicable default. The bundled
reference is never counted as a personal recording. Lower cutoffs include only
time to that cutoff. Missing inputs or insufficient current leaves ETA absent.
Taper, temperature and load can affect the result; it is not a promised finish
time or independent battery-capacity measurement. Estimates end at displayed
100%; battery balancing may continue afterward.

Export charge recordings writes versioned JSON for the selected scooter using
Android's save picker, including original readings, timestamps, gaps, stop reason
and manual charger label. Capture must stop before export. Owner credentials,
names, MAC addresses, PINs and location are excluded.

New charge samples also retain the original full/limit time estimates, limit,
estimator version and source (live rate, stock reference or personal curve).
More → Developer options → Charge recordings shows Time remaining and Predicted
finish graphs with full-resolution touch inspection and fullscreen views. An
observed 100% finish provides a reference and prediction error; incomplete
recordings and lower cutoffs are never compared to a guessed completion time.
Older journals have no historical predictions; estimates are not backfilled.
