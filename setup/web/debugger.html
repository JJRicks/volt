Need help recovering?

The phone’s own timer attempts cleanup ten minutes after recording started. If recovery fails, wait ten minutes and try again. Don’t start Volt’s pairing helper or another capture tool, and don’t clear this site’s data.

On the phone, Settings → Apps → NAVEE → Force stop closes NAVEE, but doesn’t by itself restore the log setting. The recovery file below contains no scooter key or log lines; share it only with someone you trust to help.

02 / GET READY

You’ll need:

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge.
  • A USB data cable for your Android phone.
  • USB debugging turned on (how-to below).
  • NAVEE signed in and able to connect to your scooter.
  • Your scooter nearby, parked and switched on.
During the recording

Don’t change your handlebar PIN, NAVEE password or account. Those lines are never kept, but it’s safest not to make them at all.

How to turn on USB debugging
  1. On your phone, open Settings → About phone. Tap Build number seven times. On Samsung, open Software information first.
  2. Go back to Settings → System (or the main list) → Developer options, and turn on USB debugging.
  3. Connect the cable and unlock your phone.

Only the main personal Android user is supported; work profiles and secondary users are not.

03 / CONNECT

Connect your phone.

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect your phone with a USB data cable and unlock it.

Connection help
  1. Close other scooter apps, Android Studio, scrcpy, and other USB setup tabs. If ADB is installed on this computer, run adb kill-server in a terminal: its background server keeps the phone busy after adb devices finishes.
  2. Unplug and reconnect the phone, unlock it, and try again.

This page cannot connect to the phone it’s running on. On Linux, the browser needs Android udev rules (Ubuntu or Debian: android-sdk-platform-tools-common); Snap or Flatpak browsers may not see USB phones. Windows may need your phone’s USB driver; a work-managed phone may block debugging.

Approve access to your phone

USB debugging gives this page broad developer access. Use only a phone and scooter you own or have permission to use.

Use a regular browser window, not Incognito or Guest. Keep its site data until cleanup finishes.

On the phone prompt, leave “Always allow” unchecked.

Select your phone, then approve its USB debugging prompt.

04 / RECORD

Ready to record.

Android phone connected

We’ll restart NAVEE with temporary logging on. You use NAVEE as long as you need (up to nine minutes), then press Stop. We close NAVEE and restore its log setting.

What’s recorded?

Only NAVEE’s own Bluetooth and device-control lines are kept, such as the commands it sends your scooter. Network, account, login and payment lines, and anything we don’t recognize, are hidden and only counted.

Scooter keys, PIN commands, Bluetooth addresses, serial numbers, email addresses and phone numbers are removed. Nothing is sent until you review every line and choose Send.

A cleanup timer also runs on the phone. If the cable comes out or this tab closes, return here with the same phone to verify cleanup.
