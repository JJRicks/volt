Get Volt

Your scooter. Your connection.

Volt is an independent, open source Android app for your NAVEE scooter. Lock and unlock, change settings, watch live readings, ride in full screen and record rides, straight over Bluetooth. No account, and it works offline.

Download for Android How to set up

Free · Android 8.0 and newer · Which NAVEE models?

Controls and readings that work anywhere.

The official NAVEE app requires an internet connection to work. Volt talks to the scooter directly over Bluetooth, so the controls and numbers you want are there with or without signal.

Lock and unlock without a signal

A slide-to-lock switch that talks straight to your scooter. Volt shows the change once the scooter confirms it.

Slide to lock Slide to unlock

A dashboard you arrange

Move and resize widgets so what you check most comes first. On the road, tap RIDE for a full-screen view with big speed and range at this pace.

Live readings and history

Battery, range, power, temperatures and trip stats, with charts and history.

Ride settings

Top speed, cruise control, energy recovery, lights and more, for the controls your model supports.

Every ride, mapped by power or grade.

Record a ride and see the route colored by power or grade. Rides end on their own when you switch the scooter off. While it charges, a live card on your lock screen shows the level, power and finish time.

Set up once, then ride.

You'll need:

  • Android 8.0+ with Bluetooth
  • An activated NAVEE scooter
  • The NAVEE app, signed in and connecting
  • For pairing once: a computer with USB, or Shizuku

  1. Install

    Download the APK from GitHub Releases and open it on your phone.

  2. Pair once

    Volt's onboarding helps you copy your scooter's owner key from the NAVEE app. You never enter a password. Open the setup helper

  3. Ride

    Close NAVEE and connect in Volt. After setup, no computer or signal needed.

Questions

Is Volt made by NAVEE?

No. Volt is an independent project and isn't affiliated with NAVEE or its app vendor.

Which scooters does it work with?

Volt is built for NAVEE scooters, with controls tailored to the connected model. Features vary by model and firmware. The US UT5 MAX has been tested on hardware; other mapped models are still being verified.

Does Volt need the internet?

No. Locking, controls, readings and ride recording work offline. Map backgrounds are optional and only load after you turn them on.

Which languages does Volt speak?

English, plus Spanish, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), Swedish, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Turkish and Polish, all in beta. Volt follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and newer, More › App preferences › Language can switch Volt alone back to English.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not right now. Volt runs on Android 8.0 and newer, including foldables.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. You can export rides or make a password-protected backup whenever you like.
